About Prime 17 Jags

We are Prime 17 Jags, a Prime Volleyball Academy club team based in Lakewood, Colorado. Bringing together multi-sport athletes from D’Evelyn High School, we support one another as we grow as volleyball players and teammates. We study hard, work hard, and dream big. Our high school team finished as the 4A state runner-up in the spring 2026 season. It is a milestone we’re proud to build on.
